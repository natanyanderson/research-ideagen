## Approval Notice

The Institutional Review Board has reviewed the above-referenced research protocol and determined that it qualifies for **EXEMPT** status under 45 CFR 46.104(d)(3). The study involves minimal risk to participants and meets the criteria for benign behavioral interventions.

Your study has been **APPROVED** to proceed under the following conditions:

1. **Conduct the study as approved.** Any modifications to the protocol, consent form, recruitment materials, or study procedures must be submitted to the IRB for review and approval before implementation.

2. **Maintain participant confidentiality.** All participant data must be stored on the university-approved encrypted cloud storage (Box) as described in the protocol. De-identification procedures must be followed prior to expert evaluation.

3. **Use only approved materials.** The following materials have been reviewed and approved:
   - Informed consent form (v1.2, dated 04/22/2026)
   - Recruitment email and flyer
   - Background and reflection surveys
   - Debrief script
   - Evaluator rubric and scoring instructions

4. **Report adverse events promptly.** Any unanticipated problems involving risk to participants must be reported to the IRB within 5 business days.

5. **Submit a continuing review or closure report** before the expiration date listed above. Studies that have not closed by their expiration date must submit a renewal application at least 30 days prior to expiration.

6. **Compensation:** $15 Amazon gift card per participant, as approved.

---

## Approved Study Parameters

- **Sample Size:** Up to 20 participants
- **Population:** CS researchers (graduate students, postdocs, faculty) with at least 1 year of active research experience
- **Recruitment:** Departmental mailing lists and posted flyers
- **Study Format:** Remote, conducted via Zoom
- **Session Duration:** Approximately 60 minutes per participant
- **Conditions:** Two-condition between-subjects (Control vs. AI-assisted)
