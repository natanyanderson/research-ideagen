# Experiment Design: Anchoring Effects in AI-Assisted Research Ideation

## Research Question
Does exposure to AI-generated research ideas before ideation anchor human creativity, reducing the originality of subsequent ideas?

## Study Design
- **Type**: Between-subjects experiment
- **Conditions**: 2
  - Control: Generate ideas without AI assistance
  - AI-Before: View AI-generated ideas first, then generate own ideas
- **Participants**: 20 CS grad students / researchers (10 per condition)
- **AI Tool**: ChatGPT (ecological validity — it's what researchers actually use)

## Measurements
Ideas scored by 2 independent expert raters on 5 criteria (1-5 each):
1. **Novelty** — How original is the core idea?
2. **Feasibility** — Can this be realistically executed?
3. **Impact** — Would this meaningfully advance the field?
4. **Clarity** — Is the idea well-defined enough to act on?
5. **Grounding** — Does the idea connect to existing literature?

Inter-rater reliability: Cohen's kappa

## Pre-Survey (6 questions)
1. How often do you use AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.)? [Never / Monthly / Weekly / Daily]
2. Which AI tools have you used? [checkboxes]
3. What do you primarily use AI tools for? [free text]
4. Rate your comfort level with AI tools (1-5)
5. Have you used AI tools specifically for research ideation? [Yes/No]
6. How would you describe your attitude toward AI in research? [Skeptical / Neutral / Enthusiastic]

## Exclusion Criteria
- Prior knowledge of study hypotheses
- Involvement in study design or development
- Prior work on AI evaluation tools or automated ideation systems

## Session Timeline (~45 min total)
1. Pre-survey (5 min)
2. Orientation / task instructions (5 min)
3. Ideation task (30 min)
4. Debrief (5 min)
