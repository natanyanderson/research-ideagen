# IRB Protocol Section — Draft

## Study Protocol: AI-Assisted Research Ideation Study

### 1. Study Overview

This study investigates whether AI-assisted research ideation helps or hinders the novelty of ideas generated by researchers, with particular focus on anchoring effects introduced by AI suggestions. Participants will complete a research ideation task under one of two conditions: unaided (control) or AI-assisted using our pipeline.

### 2. Participants

- **Target sample:** 20 participants (N=20)
- **Eligibility criteria:** Active CS researchers (graduate students, postdocs, or faculty) with at least 1 year of research experience
- **Exclusion criteria:** Participants with direct prior exposure to the research ideation pipeline used in this study
- **Recruitment:** Participants will be recruited via departmental mailing lists and posted flyers. Participation is voluntary and may be withdrawn at any time without penalty.
- **Compensation:** Participants will receive a $15 Amazon gift card for completing the study (~60 minutes total)

### 3. Study Design

**Between-subjects design** with two conditions:
1. **Control:** Participant generates research ideas unaided, using only their existing knowledge and a provided literature summary
2. **AI-assisted:** Participant generates research ideas with access to the pipeline's top-3 generated ideas and their novelty scores as a starting point

Participants will be randomly assigned to conditions. Each participant completes one ideation session on a single assigned research topic, drawn from a pre-selected set of 10 CS topics spanning NLP, systems, HCI, and ML.

### 4. Procedure

1. **Consent & Briefing (5 min):** Participant reads and signs informed consent form. Researcher explains the task and answers questions. No information is shared about the study hypotheses.
2. **Background Survey (5 min):** Participant reports research area, years of experience, and familiarity with the assigned topic (1–5 scale).
3. **Ideation Task (30 min):** Participant generates as many research ideas as possible for the assigned topic within the time limit.
   - *Control:* Participant receives a 1-page literature summary of the topic (5 key papers, abstracts only) and generates ideas in a structured response form.
   - *AI-assisted:* Participant receives the same literature summary plus the pipeline’s top-3 generated ideas with novelty scores. Participant may build on, modify, or ignore the AI-generated ideas.
4. **Reflection Survey (10 min):** Participant rates their own ideas on confidence and novelty (1–5 scale), and AI-assisted participants report how much they relied on the AI suggestions.
5. **Debrief (10 min):** Study hypotheses are explained. Participant is invited to ask questions.

### 5. Data Collection & Measures

All sessions conducted remotely via Zoom. Ideation responses collected via a shared Google Form.

**Primary measures:**
- **Idea count:** Number of distinct ideas generated per participant
- **Expert novelty ratings:** Each idea scored 1–5 on the five-criterion rubric (Novelty, Feasibility, Impact, Clarity, Grounding) by three expert evaluators (CS PhD students/postdocs), blind to condition
- **Inter-rater reliability:** Cohen’s κ across evaluators; target κ > 0.5; majority voting used for final classifications

**Secondary measures:**
- Participant self-rated confidence and novelty per idea
- AI reliance rating (AI-assisted condition only)
- Ideation time per idea (logged via form timestamps)

### 6. Data Storage & Privacy

- All data stored on university-approved encrypted cloud storage (Box, provisioned through institutional IT)
- Participant responses de-identified before expert evaluation
- No personally identifiable information retained beyond consent forms, which are stored separately from study data
- Data retained for 5 years per institutional policy, then destroyed

### 7. Risks & Benefits

**Risks:** Minimal. The study involves no deception, no sensitive topics, and no physical risk. Participants may experience mild cognitive effort during the ideation task.

**Benefits:** Participants gain exposure to AI-assisted research tools and contribute to research on scientific creativity and AI augmentation. Findings may inform tools that benefit the broader research community.

### 8. Informed Consent

Participants will be provided a written consent form detailing study purpose, procedures, voluntary participation, right to withdraw, data handling, and compensation. Consent will be obtained prior to any data collection.
